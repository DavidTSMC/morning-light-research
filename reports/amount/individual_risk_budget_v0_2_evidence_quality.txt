D. LOOK-AHEAD GUARDRAIL
======================================================================================================================
The following MUST NOT determine Risk Budget at T_action:
- future threshold hit
- HitLag
- later Evidence deterioration state
- Post1D / Post3D / Post5D
- Final10D outcome

These variables are useful for validation,
but they are not available when the sizing decision is made.

E. INTERVIEW DECISION
======================================================================================================================
NO REAL-TIME A2 QUALITY VARIABLE IS STORED IN THE FROZEN v0.3R EVENT FILE.

Therefore v0.2 does NOT invent one.
Current evidence supports a COMMON BASE RISK BUDGET for A2, subject to downstream ceilings.
If differentiated A2 budgets are desired, a new prospective real-time evidence feature must first be defined and validated.
