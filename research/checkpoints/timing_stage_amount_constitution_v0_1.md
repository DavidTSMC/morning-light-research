## 5. Aging / Overcooking

Age alone is NOT an exit signal.

Observed pooled shelf-life:

with deterioration appearing in the 15D -> 20D incremental window.

Therefore:

Age tells Chef when to inspect.
Deterioration tells Chef when to act.

Working concept:

OVERCOOKING CONTEXT
=
Age × Deterioration × Stock Personality

No mechanical Day-15 sell rule.

## 8. Deployment Philosophy

Morning Light does NOT currently endorse a fixed number of entry batches
as a universal rule.

Preferred research direction:

Evidence progresses -> Deployment may progress.
Evidence deteriorates -> Deployment may reverse.

Reserve protects against being wrong.
Deployment protects against being left behind.

Too much too early creates timing / concentration risk.
Too little for too long creates opportunity cost.
