E. INTERPRETATION
====================================================================================================================
Smaller Base Risk Budget improves survival but may reduce market participation.
Larger Base Risk Budget improves deployment but consumes portfolio damage capacity faster.

This test does NOT determine the optimal budget.
It defines the damage geometry that any future budget decision must respect.

Cluster and Aggregate Portfolio ceilings remain necessary because multiple A2 theses can fail together.

Macro stress may later reduce permission, but is deliberately excluded from this test.
